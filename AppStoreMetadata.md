## Description (4000 chars max)
ReceiptVault is your personal receipt processing and expense tracking assistant. Scan paper receipts, import digital ones, or simply speak your receipt details — ReceiptVault captures and organizes everything automatically.

THREE WAYS TO CAPTURE RECEIPTS:

• Camera Scan — Point your camera at any paper receipt. Our advanced OCR technology instantly reads the vendor name, items, prices, and totals. Auto-edge detection ensures a clean scan every time.

• Digital Import — Import receipt PDFs or images from your Files app or email. You can also enter receipt details manually for maximum flexibility.

• Voice Record — Simply speak your receipt details like "Walmart, April 3rd, forty-five dollars." ReceiptVault converts your voice to text and automatically extracts the vendor, date, and amount.

SMART AI PARSING (PRO):

Upgrade to Pro for AI-powered receipt parsing that works perfectly on any receipt format — grocery stores, restaurants, gas stations, retail, and more. Our AI understands receipt layouts and extracts every detail with near-perfect accuracy.

ORGANIZED DASHBOARD:

• View spending grouped by month with per-month totals
• See which vendors you spend the most with
• Drill into any vendor to see individual receipts by month
• Running totals help you track cumulative spending
• Search any vendor instantly with month and year breakdowns

BUILT FOR SECURITY:

• Biometric lock (Face ID / Touch ID) when returning to the app
• All data encrypted in transit and at rest
• Private cloud storage — only you can access your receipts
• On-device OCR — receipt images processed locally, never sent to external servers
• Account deletion removes all data permanently

KEY FEATURES:

• Scan paper receipts with auto-edge detection
• Import PDFs and images
• Voice-to-text receipt entry
• AI-powered parsing (Pro)
• Monthly spending dashboard
• Vendor-level detail with running totals
• Full-text search by vendor name
• Swipe-to-delete receipts
• Receipt image storage and verification
• Duplicate receipt detection
• Dark mode support
